The campus is not just the engineering college.

RNSIT, its MBA and MCA, RNS International School, the PU college and the degree college all share this entrance. The school starts at 08:00 and 08:45, the PU college at 08:30, the college at 09:00. Those bells overlap, and every vehicle uses the same gate, because there is only one vehicle gate on the campus.

Intake from RNSIT’s AICTE mandatory disclosure (Oct 2025). School timings published by RNS International School, whose CBSE record gives its address as “RNSIT Campus”. Main Gate capacity of 35 a minute measured from our own footage.

This is the insight, and it only appeared once we looked at the whole campus instead of the engineering college. Six institutions share one entrance. The school bell is at eight and again at a quarter to nine, the PU college at half past eight, and the engineering college at nine. Those waves overlap. And all of it goes through one gate, because that is the only vehicle gate there is. So about fifty-three vehicles a minute arrive at a gate that clears thirty-five. That rules out the easy answer and leaves three levers that need no new road: make two lanes out of the one gate, because four in five vehicles are two-wheelers stuck behind turning cars; stagger arrivals in time; and coordinate the bells between institutions.
