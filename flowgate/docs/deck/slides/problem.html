THE PROBLEM

Twice a day, the gate chokes.

During college entry and exit, vehicles back up onto the main road, drivers circle for parking and students walk between moving bikes and buses.

DELAYS

Long gate queues

Hundreds of vehicles reach the same gate in the same few minutes.

MOVEMENT

Wasted circling

No one knows which lot has space, so drivers search inside campus.

PARKING

Lots fill unevenly

One lot overflows while another, a short walk away, sits half empty.

SAFETY

People among vehicles

Bus riders and walkers cross the gate at the busiest moment.

This is the problem statement in plain words. Four symptoms: delays, inefficient movement, parking congestion and safety. They are all caused by the same thing, which is the next slide.
