HOW IT WORKS

From timetable to gate in five steps

1

Timetable in

Admin uploads class timings for each department and year.

2

Slot optimizer

Spreads arrivals into 10-minute windows under gate capacity.

3

Student app

Shows the slot, books a parking zone and issues a QR pass.

4

Gate scan

The guard scans the QR and the driver is sent to their zone.

5

Control room

Live occupancy, gate flow, alerts and peak-hour simulation.

Gate cameras: YOLOv8 counts every vehicle

Same API as QR scans, live in step 5

The whole loop. The college uploads the timetable, our optimizer turns it into slots, students see their slot and book parking, the guard scans the pass, and the control room sees everything live. Gate cameras are a second data source: a YOLO model counts every vehicle, including people who didn’t use the app.
