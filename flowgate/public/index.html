Smart campus traffic for RNSIT

Stop everyone arriving
at the same minute.

The gates do not jam because the road is too small. They jam because almost everyone arrives in the same fifteen minutes. FlowGate spreads arrivals out, then sends each vehicle straight to a known space.

We filmed our main gate during the morning peak and counted it with the YOLOv8 script in this project. Then a person counted the same 30 seconds by hand: 20 vehicles against the software's 19, so the counts are good to about 5%. At peak a vehicle passes every 1.8 seconds, which is why nothing in FlowGate asks a vehicle to stop at the gate. Method and limits: docs/measured/

What it does

six parts, one system
Staggered slotsEach class gets its own ten-minute arrival window, built from the timetable
Predictive controlForecasts queues twenty minutes ahead and acts before one forms
Parking and passesBook a space before leaving home; the pass is checked at the parking area, not the gate
Green creditsPoints for arriving on time and carpooling unlock priority parking
Gate signageA screen at the gate: which gate to use, waits, free spaces, crossing signals
CopilotSecurity asks questions in plain English and gets a shift report
