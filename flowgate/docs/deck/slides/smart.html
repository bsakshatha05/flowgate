THE SMART GATE

Signage, hands-free entry, credits, copilot

Gate signage on any TV

The gate to use, waits, free parking, the walk signal and emergency lanes.

Hands-free entry

A plate camera checks in registered vehicles without stopping. QR stays as the fallback.

Green credits

Arriving in your slot and carpooling earn points that unlock priority parking.

Copilot for security

Ask which gate needs a marshal, or get a shift report. Claude online, rules offline.

Four features that make the gate smarter without new infrastructure for the pilot. The signage is a web page any TV with a browser can show. Plate reads are simulated in the demo; in production an ANPR camera posts to the same API. Green credits give people a reason to keep their slot. The copilot answers in plain English and only sends aggregate numbers.
