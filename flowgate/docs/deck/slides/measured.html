We checked the software by hand

A person counted the same 30 seconds: 20 vehicles. The software said 19. Good to about 5%.

This changed the design

At one vehicle every 1.8 seconds, scanning a QR code at the gate would become the jam. So the pass is checked at the parking area, and the gate stays free-flowing.

One entrance, one morning. This measures flow at that point, not campus-wide demand, and not the gate's ceiling. It does not replace the modelled 53/min, which is every institution on campus arriving at the one vehicle gate.

Everything up to here was a model. This slide is our own campus, counted by our own software, and then checked by a human: twenty against nineteen. Lead with that, because it is what makes the rest believable. Then the design point: measuring our own gate told us a QR scan per vehicle would make things worse, so we moved the check off the gate.
