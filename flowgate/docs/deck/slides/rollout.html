ROLLOUT AND FUTURE SCOPE

Cheap to start, easy to grow

WEEKS 1 TO 2

Measure

Run the camera counter on existing CCTV to get real arrival curves for each gate.

WEEKS 3 TO 4

Pilot

Stagger two departments and open parking pre-booking for their students.

MONTH 2

Whole campus

Every cohort gets a slot. Guards scan passes on a shared phone at each gate.

No new hardware for the pilot: existing CCTV, guards’ phones, one laptop, a TV for signage.

Next, after the pilot

Plate cameras with on-device OCR at each gate

Live bus GPS so riders see arrival times

Traffic police and signal timing data

EV charging slots in the booking flow

A low-risk rollout. First measure with cameras we already have, then pilot with two departments, then the whole campus. The future scope items each build on the same platform.
