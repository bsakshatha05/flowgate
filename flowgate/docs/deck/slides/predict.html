WHEN THE DAY GOES WRONG

Predict the queue, act before it forms

08:15 Gate cut to one lane

A breakdown in the gate mouth, in the rain, with only 60% keeping their slot. There is one vehicle gate, so there is nowhere to divert to.

08:15 Forecast fires

From bookings and the reported incident alone, it sees the queue coming and sends a marshal at once, four minutes before a reactive system would react.

Riders who haven’t left get a nudge

If the forecast still runs hot, the app moves riders into the minutes that genuinely have room, earlier or later, never past 08:50.

Slots handle a normal day. The real test is a bad day: rain, people ignoring their slot, and a gate blocked by an incident. Reactive FlowGate only sends a marshal at 08:19, once the queue is forming. The predictive controller acts at 08:15. It forecasts only from bookings and the reported incident, never the true demand. The nudge is now deliberately small: it only drains minutes that are actually over capacity, and only into minutes with real room. On this day it moves 58 riders, and the peak is the same with or without it, so almost all of the gain comes from the second lane and from sending the marshal early. Four minutes of warning is worth 140 vehicles here, because with one gate there is nowhere to divert to. All simulated, with the assumptions in src/config.js.
